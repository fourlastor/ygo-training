# Decks of the games of the era

Each `.ydk` here is a deck of its own, named by its file, with the generic policy as
its pilot (`crates/ygo-env`, `Environment::open`). They are the opponents' Decks of the
World Championship and Tag Force games, numbered in the order they bring new cards
(`experiments/2026-11-02-expanded-pool.md`). Keep the names neutral: the games'
character names go nowhere tracked.
